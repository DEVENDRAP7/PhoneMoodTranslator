Privacy Policy for Phone Mood Translator

Last updated: February 2026

Phone Mood Translator respects your privacy and is designed to work entirely offline without collecting any personal data.

Information Collection and Use
The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. All analysis is performed locally on your device. No account creation is required.

Usage Access Permission
Phone Mood Translator uses Android's Usage Access permission solely to analyze app usage duration in order to generate mood-based insights. This data never leaves your device and is not shared with any third party. This permission works identically to Android's built-in Digital Wellbeing feature.

Notification Permission
On Android 13 and above, the app may request notification permission to send gentle reminders when you exceed your self-set usage limits. Notifications can be disabled at any time from Android Settings.

Data Storage
All mood history, usage insights, hourly heatmap data and self-notes are stored locally in an encrypted database on your device only. The app does not use cloud storage, external servers, or analytics services. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data.

Internet Access
Phone Mood Translator does not require or use internet access. No network requests are made at any time.

Third-Party Libraries
The app uses the following open source libraries solely for local UI rendering. None of these libraries collect or transmit any data:

MPAndroidChart — chart rendering
Room Database — local data storage
Gson — local JSON parsing
AmbilWarna — color picker UI

Third-Party Services
Phone Mood Translator does not use third-party analytics, advertisements, crash reporting or tracking services of any kind.

Children's Privacy
Phone Mood Translator is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future to reflect app updates. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
